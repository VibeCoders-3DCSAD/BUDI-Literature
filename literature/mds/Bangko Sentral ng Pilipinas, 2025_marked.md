While ownership of e-money and bank accounts remained steady at 36 percentand 23 percent, respectively, a decline was more evident in loan-anchored accounts heldwith microfinance non-government organizations (NGOs) and cooperatives. Computer-assistedpersonal interviewing (CAPI) was administered, wherein the field interviewers recordedrespondent’s answers on tablets/smartphones during the face-to-face interviews. Analysisand report-writing, focusing on the financial inclusion components of the survey, wereundertaken by BSP-Financial Inclusion Office (FIO).9The survey instrument is an enhancement and consolidation of the 2021 CFS and 2021 FISquestionnaires. By merging two previously separate instruments, the questionnaire wasstreamlined to eliminate overlapping concepts and harmonize definitions. Unlike the FIS, the 2025 CFIS uses the PSA’s 2023 Geo-enabled Master Sample (GeoMS),which is based on the 2020 Census of Population and Housing (CPH). With a standardizedsampling methodology and an expanded respondent base of 8,784 Filipino adults, fromonly 1,200 respondents in the 2021 FIS, the 2025 CFIS provides more reliable estimates,including at the regional level.Further, to ensure accessibility, the questionnaire was translated into six major Philippinelanguages, namely, Filipino, Cebuano, Ilocano, Bikol, Hiligaynon, and Waray, with anEnglish translation for each question.To encourage participation, respondents received a BSP-signed letter explaining thesurvey objectives and assuring confidentiality. Public advisories were posted on the BSPwebsite and Facebook page, and endorsements were sought from the PSA, localgovernment unit, barangay offices, and Homeowners’ Associations (HOA), as needed. 10Survey Limitations The CFIS has several limitations that readers should consider: Sampling: As is common in survey research, results are still subject to samplingvariability. Questionnaire Design: The long and detailed questionnaire may cause measurementerrors due to recall issues, respondent fatigue, or misunderstanding of questionsData Quality: Since the survey relies on self-reported financial information, somerespondents may underreport or withhold sensitive details, particularly on finances.These factors can lead to non-sampling errors affecting data reliability.2025 CONSUMER FINANCE AND INCLUSION SURVEY18 https://www.psrc.com.ph/10 PSA Greenlights the Conduct of the 2023 Consumer Finance and Inclusion Survey (Released on 26 September 2024)11Sampling and Respondent SelectionThe Primary Sampling Units (PSUs) and Secondary Sampling Units (SSUs) weregenerated by PSA from the 2023 GeoMS using one (1) replicate. Within each sampledhousehold, a randomization program embedded in the CAPI system was used toselect one respondent from among household members aged 15 years or older. If there is more than one (1) household within a dwelling, the household to participatein the interview was chosen via simple random sampling. If the initial adult informantis not part of the randomly selected household, the enumerator asked to speak with amember of the selected household. Sampling Margin of ErrorThe sampling margin of error (MOE) is ±1.0% at the national level. Results can bedisaggregated by region, locality (urban, rural), sex, socio-economic class, educationalattainment, age group, and class of worker, among others.
