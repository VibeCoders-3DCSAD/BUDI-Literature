Sub-Title :

Household spending sustains growth in Q1 2025

HFCE recorded a year-on-year growth of 5.3 percent in the first quarter of 2025, an acceleration from the 4.7 percent growth

posted in the same quarter of the previous year. (Figure 5)

The  top  five  contributors  to  the  HFCE  year-on-year  growth  in  the  first  quarter  of  2025  were  Food  and  non-alcoholic

beverages  with  an  annual  growth  of  4.5  percent;  Transport,  8.5  percent;  Miscellaneous  goods  and  services,  4.7  percent;

Restaurants and hotels, 6.0 percent; and Health, 9.8 percent. All HFCE items posted growths in the said period.

Food and non-alcoholic beverages continued to account for the largest share of household consumption at 32.6 percent. This

was  followed  by  Miscellaneous  goods  and  services,  14.0  percent;  and  Housing,  water,  electricity,  gas,  and  other  fuels,  11.9

percent.

Page ID :

Sub-Title :

Household consumption slows down in Q3 2025

Household  final  consumption  expenditure  (HFCE)  grew  year-on-year  by  4.1  percent  in  the  third  quarter  of  2025.  This  was

slower than the 5.2 percent growth recorded in the same quarter of 2024. (Figure 5)

The top five contributors to HFCE growth in the third quarter of 2025 were Food and non-alcoholic beverages, 4.2 percent;

Health,  11.3  percent;  Miscellaneous  goods  and  services,  3.7  percent;  Restaurants  and  hotels,  5.9  percent;  and  Transport,

4.4  percent.  On  the  other  hand,  the  subsectors  that  posted  year-on-year  declines  in  the  same  quarter  were  Alcoholic

beverages and tobacco; Furnishings, household equipment and routine household maintenance; and Clothing and footwear,

which posted declines of 7.5 percent, 0.8 percent, and 0.4 percent, respectively.

Food and non-alcoholic beverages remained the largest component of household spending at 32.8 percent of the total HFCE.

This was followed by Miscellaneous goods and services at 15.2 percent, and Housing, water, electricity, gas, and other fuels at

11.9 percent.
