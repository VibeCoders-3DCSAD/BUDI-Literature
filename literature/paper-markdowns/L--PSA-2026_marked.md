Sub-Title :

Household spending eases in Q1 2026

HFCE grew year-on-year by 3.0 percent in the first quarter of 2026, slower than the 5.3 percent growth recorded in the same

quarter of 2025. (Figure 5)

The  top  contributors  to  the  growth  of  HFCE  were  Miscellaneous  goods  and  services,  4.0  percent;  Restaurants  and  hotels,

4.2 percent; and Education, 6.7 percent. All other HFCE items posted growth during the period.

Food and non-alcoholic beverages accounted for the largest share to the total HFCE at 31.9 percent. This was followed by

Miscellaneous goods and services at 14.1 percent; and Housing, water, electricity, gas and other fuels at 11.9 percent.

Sub-Title :

Household spending slows down in Q2 2026

Household  final  consumption  expenditure  (HFCE)  increased  year-on-year  by  2.8  percent  in  the  second  quarter  of  2026,

slower than the 5.2 percent growth recorded in the same quarter of 2025. (Figure 5)

The top contributors to the growth of HFCE were Food and non-alcoholic beverages, 2.4 percent; Miscellaneous goods and

services, 5.8 percent; and Education, 13.0 percent. Meanwhile, the growth of HFCE was constrained by the contractions in the

following:  Transport,  -7.5  percent;  Alcoholic  beverages,  tobacco,  -1.9  percent;  Recreation  and  culture,  -0.8  percent;  and

Restaurants and hotels, -0.2 percent.

Food and non-alcoholic beverages accounted for the largest share to the total HFCE at 36.3 percent. This was followed by

Housing, water, electricity, gas and other fuels, 15.0 percent; and Miscellaneous goods and services, 13.8 percent.
