# Credit Card Profitability

## Summary

Using regulatory credit-card data for 13 large banks from 2014 to 2021, the authors decompose credit-card profitability into a transaction function, a credit function, and fees. The credit function supplies about 80 percent of profitability, fees about 15 percent, and the transaction function is slightly negative once rewards and other expenses are counted. Revolvers, especially heavy revolvers, pay most of the interest and a large share of the fees.

## Method

**Design.** Descriptive decomposition of regulatory credit-card data; the authors state no formal research design label.
**Sample.** 13 stress-tested banks in a constant sample, with monthly portfolio and account-level data from January 2014 to December 2021; Table 1 summarizes about 306 million accounts.

## Limitations and Gaps

- The analysis is descriptive and covers the largest stress-tested banks, so it describes that segment rather than the whole credit-card market and does not estimate causal effects.
