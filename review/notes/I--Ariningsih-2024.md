## Summary

This study evaluates the quality of a ticketing management system using the 2023 edition of ISO/IEC 25010 and the Analytic Hierarchy Process. The authors assess product quality characteristics and compute weighted results to determine overall system quality.

## Problem and Motivation

Organizations need systematic ways to evaluate software quality. The study applies ISO/IEC 25010:2023 and AHP to evaluate a ticketing management system to ensure it meets quality expectations.

## Method

**Design.** Quality evaluation study combining ISO/IEC 25010:2023 product quality model with AHP for weighting.  
**Sample.** Not reported (case study of a ticketing management system; respondents/evaluators not fully specified in the available text).  
**Context.** Geography: Indonesia; population: users/stakeholders of the ticketing management system; setting: ticketing management system evaluation.

- Define evaluation criteria based on ISO/IEC 25010:2023 product quality characteristics.
- Apply AHP to determine weights of quality characteristics and sub-characteristics.
- Collect evaluation data from relevant respondents.
- Calculate weighted scores and assess overall quality of the ticketing system.

## Key Findings

- The study demonstrates applying ISO/IEC 25010:2023 with AHP to evaluate software quality of a ticketing management system.
- Weighted evaluation results are used to assess the system against the quality model.
