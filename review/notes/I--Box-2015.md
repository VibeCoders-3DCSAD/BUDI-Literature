`I--Box-2015` — Box, Jenkins, Reinsel, and Ljung (2015), *Time Series Analysis: Forecasting and Control* (5th ed.), Wiley.

## Summary

The fifth edition of the canonical Box-Jenkins text covers time series analysis, forecasting, and control. It provides the theoretical and practical foundation for ARIMA and seasonal ARIMA (SARIMA) model identification (ACF/PACF), parameter estimation, diagnostic checking, and forecasting. This is the 5th edition (2015); Chapter 2 cites the 1970 1st edition.

## Problem and Motivation

To provide a systematic methodology for building stochastic models for time series data and using them for forecasting and process control.

- Establishes the iterative Box-Jenkins methodology for ARIMA model building.
