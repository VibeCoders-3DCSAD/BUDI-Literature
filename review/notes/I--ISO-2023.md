## Summary

ISO/IEC 25010:2023 defines the product quality model in the SQuaRE series. It specifies quality characteristics and sub-characteristics for systems and software products. The 2023 edition updates the model from the 2011 version.

## Problem and Motivation

Provides a standardized framework for specifying and evaluating software product quality.
