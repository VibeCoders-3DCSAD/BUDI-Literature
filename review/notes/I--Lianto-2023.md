## Summary

This Indonesian study evaluates the VR Gamelan Slenthem application using four characteristics of ISO/IEC 25010:2011 (functional suitability, performance efficiency, usability, and portability). The evaluation assesses whether the application meets quality standards for educational use.

## Problem and Motivation

Educational VR applications need quality evaluation to ensure functionality, performance, usability, and portability. The study applies ISO/IEC 25010 to evaluate the VR Gamelan Slenthem application.
