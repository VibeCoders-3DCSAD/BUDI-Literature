## Summary

This systematic review identifies and analyzes usability questionnaires used in studies of standalone or interactive mobile health (mHealth) applications. The review reports which instruments are most frequently used, how they are applied, and their characteristics. The System Usability Scale appears among the commonly used questionnaires.

## Problem and Motivation

There is no consensus on which usability questionnaire to use for mHealth apps. The review synthesizes existing practices to inform selection of appropriate usability measures.

## Method

**Design.** Systematic review.  
**Sample.** Studies evaluating usability of standalone or interactive mobile health applications using questionnaires.  
**Context.** Geography: Not reported (global); population: mHealth app users/studies; setting: mobile health applications.

- Systematically search for studies using usability questionnaires for mHealth apps.
- Extract questionnaire names, frequency of use, and study characteristics.
- Synthesize findings on instrument usage patterns.

## Key Findings

- Multiple usability questionnaires are used in mHealth research; SUS is among the frequently used instruments.
- The review provides a mapping of questionnaires to mHealth contexts.

## Limitations and Gaps

- Review based on published studies; reporting quality varies across included studies.
