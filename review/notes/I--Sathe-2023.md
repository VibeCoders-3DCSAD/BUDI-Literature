## Summary

This empirical study investigates the impact of project management constraints on agile software development, comparing Scrum and Kanban using multigroup analysis. The paper examines how constraints affect agile projects under different methodologies.

## Problem and Motivation

Agile methodologies are widely adopted, but project management constraints can impact outcomes. The study compares how Scrum and Kanban respond to project management constraints.
