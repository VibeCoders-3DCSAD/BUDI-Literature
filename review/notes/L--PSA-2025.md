# Household Final Consumption Expenditure (HFCE) — 2025 quarterly press releases (Q1–Q4)

`L--PSA-2025` — Philippine Statistics Authority (2025), *Philippine Statistics Authority* \[Not reported]

## Summary

The series entry concatenates the four quarterly HFCE press releases of the Philippine Statistics Authority for 2025. Household final consumption expenditure grew 5.3% year-on-year in Q1 2025, 5.5% in Q2, 4.1% in Q3, and 3.8% in Q4, for full-year growth of 4.6%. Food and non-alcoholic beverages remained the largest spending category (32.6% of household consumption in Q1) and the top contributor to HFCE growth in every quarter.

## Problem and Motivation

The releases track quarterly household final consumption — the largest component of aggregate demand in the Philippines (about 75% of the economy) — providing the official series the design wants to decompose into seasonal proportions.

## Method

**Design.** Quarterly statistical press release series on the System of National Accounts (Gross Domestic Product) for the Philippines.
**Sample.** Not applicable (institutional statistical series; official national-accounts estimates, not a survey sample).
**Context.** geography: Philippines (national); population: national economy; setting: PSA national accounts, released at the household final consumption expenditure (HFCE) component of GDP.

- Each release reports quarter-on-quarter year-on-year HFCE growth (Figure 5 accompanying each release) and the top five contributors to that growth by commodity group.
- Releases report the share of total household consumption held by each commodity group (e.g., Food and non-alcoholic beverages 32.6% in Q1).
- The Q4 release adds the full-year growth figure and HFCE's share of the total economy.
- Figures are year-on-year growth rates; the releases do not publish seasonally adjusted values or seasonal factors directly.

## Software

Not reported (statistical dissemination; seasonally adjusted series are published separately by PSA on openSTAT).

## Key Findings

- num: HFCE grew 5.3% year-on-year in Q1 2025, accelerating from the 4.7% growth in Q1 2024 (Q1 release, page 1).
- num: HFCE grew 5.5% year-on-year in Q2 2025, higher than the 4.8% posted in Q2 2024 (Q2 release, page 4).
- num: HFCE grew 4.1% year-on-year in Q3 2025, slower than the 5.2% growth in Q3 2024 (Q3 release, page 7).
- num: HFCE grew 3.8% year-on-year in Q4 2025, slower than the 4.7% in Q4 2024 (Q4 release, page 10).
- num: For full-year 2025, HFCE expanded by 4.6% (Q4 release, page 10).
- num: Top five contributors to Q1 2025 growth: Food and non-alcoholic beverages 4.5%, Transport 8.5%, Miscellaneous goods and services 4.7%, Restaurants and hotels 6.0%, Health 9.8%; all HFCE items posted growth (Q1 release, page 1).
- num: Food and non-alcoholic beverages were the largest spending share at 32.6%, followed by Miscellaneous goods and services 14.0% and Housing, water, electricity, gas, and other fuels 11.9% (Q1 release, page 1).
- num: In Q4 2025, Food and non-alcoholic beverages accounted for the largest share with 35.7% and contributed 3.8% to the quarter's growth (Q4 release, page 10).
- num: HFCE comprised about 75% of the total economy in Q4 2025 (75.3% vs 74.7% in Q4 2024) (Q4 release, page 10).

## Key Figures and Tables

- Contributor tables ranking commodity groups by their contribution to HFCE year-on-year growth.

## Limitations and Gaps

- The releases report year-on-year growth and quarter shares only; they do not publish seasonally adjusted values or seasonal factors. The design's use of HFCE "seasonal proportions" therefore relies on computing seasonality from these series (or from the seasonally adjusted openSTAT tables) rather than reading a stated factor directly off the release.
- [unacknowledged] The Q4 release says HFCE "comprised of 75.3 percent in the fourth quarter of 2024, from 74.7 percent in the same quarter of 2024" — the release text names the same quarter for both figures, which is internally inconsistent; the intended contrast is Q4 2025 (75.3%) versus Q4 2024 (74.7%). The change-off is inferred, not stated.
- Series entry: the conversion is a concatenation of four separate quarterly press releases; there is no single aggregate PDF. Citation-locator precision down to the individual release is preserved by the page markers (Q1 pages 1–3, Q2 pages 4–6, Q3 pages 7–9, Q4 pages 10–12).

## Definitions

- **HFCE (Household Final Consumption Expenditure)** — The household component of gross domestic product measuring expenditure by resident households on goods and services for final consumption.
- **Year-on-year growth** — Growth of a quarter against the same quarter of the previous year.
