# Household Final Consumption Expenditure (HFCE) — 2026 quarterly press releases (Q1–Q2)

`L--PSA-2026` — Philippine Statistics Authority (2026), *Philippine Statistics Authority* \[Not reported]

## Summary

The series entry concatenates the Philippine Statistics Authority's HFCE releases for 2026 available at capture time (Q1 and Q2). Household final consumption expenditure grew 3.0% year-on-year in Q1 2026 and 2.8% in Q2 2026, a slowdown from 5.3% in Q1 2025. Food and non-alcoholic beverages remained the largest share of total HFCE at 31.9% in Q1.

## Problem and Motivation

The releases track the largest component of Philippine aggregate demand, providing the most recent official consumption series the design uses to derive seasonal proportions for household expense forecasting.

## Method

**Design.** Quarterly statistical press release series on the System of National Accounts for the Philippines.
**Sample.** Not applicable (official national-accounts estimates, not a survey sample).
**Context.** geography: Philippines (national); population: national economy; setting: PSA national accounts, HFCE component of GDP.

- Q1 and Q2 releases each report year-on-year HFCE growth, the top growth contributors by commodity group, and per-category shares of total HFCE.
- The releases do not publish seasonally adjusted values or seasonal factors directly.

## Software

Not reported (statistical dissemination; seasonally adjusted series are published separately by PSA on openSTAT).

## Key Findings

- num: HFCE grew 3.0% year-on-year in Q1 2026, slower than the 5.3% growth in Q1 2025 (Q1 release, page 1).
- num: HFCE grew 2.8% year-on-year in Q2 2026, a continued slowdown from the 5.3% recorded in Q1 2025 (Q2 release, page 3).
- num: Top contributors to Q1 2026 growth: Miscellaneous goods and services 4.0%, Restaurants and hotels 4.2%, Education 6.7%; all other HFCE items posted growth (Q1 release, page 1).
- num: Food and non-alcoholic beverages accounted for the largest share of total HFCE at 31.9% in Q1 2026 (Q1 release, page 1).

## Limitations and Gaps

- Series is partial at capture time: only Q1 and Q2 of 2026 were released; the 2026 series will be appended with later releases. Any 2026 full-year figure is not yet reported.
- The releases report year-on-year growth and shares only; seasonality must be derived rather than read off the release.
- Series entry: the conversion concatenates the individual 2026a (Q1) and 2026b (Q2) releases (pages 1–2 and 3–5 of the conversion respectively).

## Definitions

- **HFCE (Household Final Consumption Expenditure)** — The household component of gross domestic product measuring expenditure by resident households on goods and services for final consumption.
- **Year-on-year growth** — Growth of a quarter against the same quarter of the previous year.
