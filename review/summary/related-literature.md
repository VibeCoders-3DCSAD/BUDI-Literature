| Tukey (1977). Exploratory Data Analysis. | Tukey's *Exploratory Data Analysis* introduces a set of robust, graphical, and numerical methods for summarizing data. Key contributions include stem-and-leaf displays, the five-number summary (minimum, lower hinge, median, upper hinge, maximum), hinges as robust alternatives to quartiles, and the box-and-whisker plot. These methods are foundational for robust measures of spread such as the interquartile range based on hinges. | Inter-Quartile Range |
| Brooke (1996). SUS - A quick and dirty usability scale. | John Brooke introduces the System Usability Scale (SUS), a 10-item Likert-scale questionnaire designed for quick, low-cost global assessments of system usability. SUS produces a single composite score from 0-100 and is intended to be used across a wide range of contexts. | Software Quality Evaluation |
| Hyndman (2007). Minimum Sample Size Requirements for Seasonal Forecasting Models. | This practice article answers the common business question of how much historical data are needed to forecast with a seasonal model. Hyndman and Kostenko show that the minimum sample size depends on the number of parameters in the seasonal model and the signal-to-noise ratio, and they document specific minimum requirements for common seasonal forecasting models. The article warns that published tables of minimum sample sizes are often overly simplified. | Seasonal Auto-Regressive Integrated Moving Average |
